### Quantization of Charge
Any electrical charge is an integer multiple of the **elementary charge $e$**.
$$
e=1.602\times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}
$$
EX: A particle could have charge $-6e$ or $10e$ but not $3.55e$.

The elementary charge is that of an electron (-) or a proton (+).

### Conservation of Charge
Charge is a conserved quantity

In atomic decay:
- Of course the number of protons and electrons are the same before and after, so charge is conserved
In annihilation:
- Positrons and electrons can annihilate because their net charge is 0 ($-e+e=0e$).
- In pair production, a gamma ray becomes an electron and a positron, so the net charge is still zero.
